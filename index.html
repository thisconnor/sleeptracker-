Sleep Check-in

Your sleep, decoded

One glance every morning: how much sleep you owe your body, how today's energy will flow, and exactly what to do about tonight.

Sleep debt, not sleep scores

Your last 14 nights are weighed — recent ones count most — into a single number. Keep it under five hours and your days feel like yours. Naps pay it down; sleeping in offsets it.

A plan for tonight, every morning

You get a recommended bedtime that gently repays debt, your last-caffeine time, and an energy schedule with the best window for a nap — tuned to your calendar if you link one.

Track it your way

Sync Apple Health with one tap of an iPhone Shortcut, or use the live timer: tap when you lie down, tap when you wake, confirm, done. Every night is editable — add naps, fix times.

Private by design

Your sleep data belongs to your account alone, check-in links keep it out of reach of any server, and calendar access never leaves your browser. No ads, no tracking, no subscription.
